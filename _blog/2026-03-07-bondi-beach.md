# Bondi Beach

Bondi Beach. The name comes from the Dharawal language, translating to
“thud”, relating to the noises of waves hitting the rocks.

![Bondi Beach][bondibeach]

Here we are, enjoying a sunny day at the beach.

![Selfie at Bondi Beach][bondiselfie]

## Deep Water

A serial killer preyed on gay men around this beach in the 80s. It's
thought that 70+ murders took place. A good miniseries called [*Deep
Water*][deepwater] chronicled the work of investigators on the
still-unsolved case. Yael Stone stars in the series and does not have
to pull off an Italian-American accent as she did for *Orange Is The
New Black*, as Lorna Morello.

## Speedo Cafe

The Netflix docuseries *Somebody Feed Phil* follows Phil Rosenthal
(creator of the television series *Everybody Loves Raymond*) around the
globe as he samples foods, explores places and highlights charities. In
episode five of season eight, Phil visited Australia. He enjoyed
breakfast at [Speedo Café][speedocafe], situated above the beach with a
spectacular view. Called the world's most Instagrammable cafe, we had
to contribute to the hype with our own pic. The cafe's decor celebrates
swimming, particularly the racing suit from the Australian company
Speedo. Yes, that's a boomerang as the logo.

Look at the line, though! We decided to skip eating a meal there.
