![][toll]

[Halfpenny Bridge][hpb] crosses the Thames at Lechlade. Built around 1792, it
takes its name from the toll charged to pedestrians: half a penny. The stone
toll house still stands at one end of the bridge. Nowadays, taxes collected
locally pay for the bridge upkeep, with no cost to cross it.

![][boats]

Narrowboats line the river here, on the highest stretch of the Thames that
boats can still navigate.
