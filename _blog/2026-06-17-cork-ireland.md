![Ellis Island Family][p1]

On December 20, 1891, [Anna "Annie" Moore][wwwaam], along with her brothers
Anthony and Philip, left Cork on the SS Nevada. Annie became the first person
admitted to the United States of America through the immigration center on
[Ellis Island][wwwei], on January 1, 1892.

Annie lived impoverished, with six of her eleven children passing away before
the age of three. She died from heart failure at fifty, in 1924, and ended up
buried in an unmarked Queens gravesite. Her body was identified in 2006 and a
dedication took place a couple of years later. Some urban myths exist, with
one claiming that she died in Texas, run over by a streetcar (that was a
different Annie Moore, though).

# Kinsale

In Irish, Cionn tSáile means "head of the brine", and in English we spell that
as Kinsale. Just south of Cork, at the mouth of the River Bandon, Kinsale is
home to just shy of 6,000 people. The town as a Michelin-starred restaurant,
[Bastion][wwwbrk], and welcomes tourists from around the world for its food
and music festivals.

The infamous pirate Anne Bonny came from Kinsale, born in 1700. With a career
as a pirate of only 61 days, she sailed with Calico Jack, attacking merchant
ships in the Caribbean. The entire crew captured in the summer of 1720, Bonny
escaped execution by claiming that she was pregnant. Popular stories claim
that Bonny lived an uneventful life after her prison release in Jamaica, until
her death in 1733. Other stories claim that she died in prison in 1721. In
popular culture, Bonny appears in several novels, songs, shows and films. Her
character appears in the video game *Assassin's Creed IV* and the television
show *Black Sails*.
