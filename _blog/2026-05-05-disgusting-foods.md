# Disgusting Foods

Okay, you gotta visit Berlin's [Disgusting Food Museum][dfmwww]. A short
walk from [Checkpoint Charlie][ccpost], this small museum has exhibits on all
sorts of cheeses, meats and other things that will turn any stomach. They
even have a tasting area with a sign to keep count of vomiters.

**Vomit Count**

![Vomit Count][vc]

**Canned Goods (Or Bads)**

![Canned Goods][cg]

Disgusting or not, they do point out some animals eaten only in a few points
on the planet, like pangolin.

**Pangolin Stew**

![Pangolin Stew][pang]

The most disgusting, to me, is definitely surströmming (Swedish for "sour
herring"). Fermented herring in a can, with only enough salt to keep the fish
from rotting: the *smell*. I have never smelled anything worse than that and
I certainly could not bring myself to taste it.
