Back in January 2010, Anjali and I stayed two nights in Stellenbosch, at
a place called Sylvan Vale Vineyards. The wine country, all of the little
towns and rolling hills, remains one of the most scenic drives I have
ever experienced.

## Sylvan Value Part Deux

It took us a bit of time to find the place Anjali and I had stayed at
sixteen years prior, but worth it. We enjoyed the grounds and a very nice
wine tasting. Fun to show our good friends and neighbors a spot we had
visited many years ago.

![Sylvan Vale](https://www.bandarji.com/images/sjeblog-southafrica-07-sylvanvale.jpeg)

## Sculpture Garden

We visited the [Dylan Lewis Sculpture Garden](https://www.dylanlewis.com/garden).
Cool, but hot. Very, very hot. We must have picked the record-high-temp day
to walk around in the sun, looking at art.

![Dylan Lewis Sculpture Garden](https://www.bandarji.com/images/sjeblog-southafrica-05-sculpturegarden.jpeg)

## Neethlingshof

To cool off, the four of us visited [Neethlingshof Estate Wine](https://neethlingshof.co.za/).
Established in **1692**, this place knows a thing or two because it's seen a
thing or two. Wait, wrong industry. This place knows how to make wine.

![Neethlingshof](https://www.bandarji.com/images/sjeblog-southafrica-06-neethlingshof.jpeg)
