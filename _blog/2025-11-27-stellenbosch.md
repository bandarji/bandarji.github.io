Back in January 2010, Anjali and I stayed two nights in Stellenbosch, at
a place called Sylvan Vale Vineyards. The wine country, all of the little
towns and rolling hills, remains one of the most scenic drives I have
ever experienced.

## Sylvan Value Part Deux

It took us a bit of time to find the place Anjali and I had stayed at
sixteen years prior, but worth it. We enjoyed the grounds and a very nice
wine tasting. Fun to show our good friends and neighbors a spot we had
visited many years ago.

![Sylvan Vale][sylvanvale]

## Sculpture Garden

We visited the [Dylan Lewis Sculpture Garden][dylanlewis]. Cool, but hot.
Very, very hot. We must have picked the record-high-temp day to walk around
in the sun, looking at art.

![Dylan Lewis Sculpture Garden][sculpturegarden]

## Neethlingshof

To cool off, the four of us visited [Neethlingshof Estate
Wine][neethlingshof]. Established in **1692**, this place knows a thing or
two because it's seen a thing or two. Wait, wrong industry. This place
knows how to make wine.

![Neethlingshof][neethlingshofjpg]
