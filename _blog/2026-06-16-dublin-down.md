**Obelisk**

![Obelisk][p4]

Wellington Monument sits in Dublin's Phoenix Park. The tallest obelisk in
Europe, it climbs toward the sky topping 203 feet. James Joyce's novel,
*Finnegans Wake*, references the obelisk multiple times. An urban legend
claims that a chamber exists beneath the structure where crazy dinner parties
and occult meetings have taken place.

**Colorful Doors**

![Pink Door][p6]

Dublin homes often have colorful front doors. Because residents could not
legally paint the buildings, they instead individualized their houses by
painting their doors distinct colors. Over the years, folklore has provided
multiple origin stories for the doors.

- Red doors would ward off evil.
- Bright colors helped drunken men return to the correct house after a night
of pub crawling. One story claims that writer George Moore painted his door
green so that his neighbor, Oliver St John Gogarty, would stop entering the
wrong address. In response, Gogarty painted his door red.
- Queen Victoria ordered all doors painted black after the death of Prince
Albert. Dubliners painted their doors bright colors in protest.
