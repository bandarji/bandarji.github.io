We visited and spent only one day in Northern Ireland. Instead of exploring
the city of Belfast, we opted to rent a car and drive through the countryside.

One thing I found interesting about Northern Ireland is how much the people
there have squabbled over so many things, including what to name the country
and what flag to fly.

Alternative country names include Ulster, The Province, The North, The Six
Counties and Na Sé Chontae (The Six Counties, in Irish). Northern Ireland
flies the Union Jack because the country has historically argued over flag
design without resolution. Designs based on the [Ulster Banner][wwwub] gained
some ground at one point, but ultimately failed ratification. Some supported a
design based on [Saint Patrick's Cross][wwwspc]. Others have brought up a
design based on the [Starry Plough][wwwsp]. A recreation of the traditional
Irish [Sunburst Flag][wwwsf] has come up over the years. A
[long list of flags][wwwllof] have flown in some capacity over Northern
Ireland at one point or another.

Me? I like the flag that [Suzy Eddie Izzard][wwwsei] proposed. Back in 2009,
Izzard completed 43 marathons in 51 days, for charity. When running in the
United Kingdom, Izzard carried the flag for each nation. Dismayed that
Northern Ireland did not officially adopt a country flag, Izzard created one
consisting of a green background with a white dove in the upper-right
quadrant. The dove symbolized peace, carrying an olive branch in its beak. I
based this page's theme on [that flag][wwwseif].

# Giant's Causeway

Approximately 40,000 basalt columns comprise the [Giant's Causeway][wwwgc], a
UNESCO World Heritage Site. To get there from Belfast, we drove along the
Antrim Coast roads, through a few small towns. The natural beauty of the
coastal drive had us pull over more than once.

# Game Of Thrones

We stopped in Larrybane, but decided against hiking to the abandoned limestone
quarry used as a film location for Game of Thrones. We also skipped crossing
the rope bridge to Carrick-a-Rede Island.
