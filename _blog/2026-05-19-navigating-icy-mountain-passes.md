**Ice and Snow**

![][ice04]

Just like the Led Zeppelin song sings:

> We come from the land of the ice and snow

Well, the band's song referred to Iceland, though it constantly came to mind
when peering out at the endless ice and snow.

**Markers Lead The Way**

![][ice03]

The mountains contained posts and other markers to keep caravans carrying
good on the right track. The paths through the mountains would claim the lives
of travelers under good conditions, so having people veer from the best way
to go would increase the odds for catastrophe.

![][ice02]

It would not hurt to have a cross top some of the markers, thought some of
the area's Christians.

**Warmed Up A Bit**

![][ice05]

They told me not to expect the days to get much warmer. In fact, the winds
that whip through the mountains bring the chill to unbearable levels following
even the warmest days.

Okay, moving on.
