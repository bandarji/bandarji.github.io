# Flying Within Australia

Flying from Sydney to Adelaide was an experience. Turns out, airport
security and the airlines do not care *what* you carry on board domestic
flights. They only seem to carry about dimensions and weight.

# Water

We drove down to [Sellicks Beach][sellicks], about thirty miles from
Adelaide. You can drive on the beach sand, so we parked for a bit. Anjali
got her feet wet in the water.

![Anjali on the Beach][beach]

# City

In Adelaide, we did a bit of shopping. I picked up some vinyl at [Clarity
Records][clarity]. We had lunch downtown before heading back into the
mountains.
