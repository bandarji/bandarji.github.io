# SydHarb, And Other Measurements

One of the world's deepest natural harbors, Port Jackson / Sydney Harbor,
exceeds 150 feet at one point. The harbor has ended up a unit of measure,
with one SydHarb ending up equal to 562 gigalitres. Australians, to make
that volume more relatable, say that the amount of water in the harbor
equals 238,000 Olympic swimming pools.

Australians also have another strange form of measurement, this time for
length. In a fun bit of slang, some refer to a short distance as a "bee's
dick."

For a more "mad" series of measurements, even hardcore computer nerds
might not know that famed computer scientist [Donald Knuth][knuth] came
up with the [potrzebie][potrzebie], equal to the thickness of Mad
Magazine Issue 26. Mad published part of the 19 year-old's "Potrzebie
System of Weights and Measures", which detailed volume in ngongn, mass
in blintz, power in whatmeworry, time in cowznofski, among other
important things to quantify. Makes me wonder how many hoos and hahs it
took for Knuth to write [TeX][tex].

# Car Crash

Called "Still Life With Stone And Car", this art installation on a
Sydney roundabout shows a two-ton quartz smashing a Ford Fiesta.
Because of the interpretations offered, the online explanations
regarding its significance and meaning took me down a comical rabbit
hole that lasted hours.

![Car Crash][carcash]
