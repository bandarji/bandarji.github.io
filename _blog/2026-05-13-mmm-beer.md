# Mmm, Beer

![Windmill][w]

We hoped off a local Amsterdam train, just to look around for a while. Not
far from the stop, we spotted a windmill. A closer look and we discovered
that the windmill houses a brewery. So, it was time to enjoy a beer, or two.

![Glass][g]

The motto at [Brouwerij 't IJ][b]: *Live with your head held high*.
