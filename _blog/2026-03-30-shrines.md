# Shrines

Some Japanese shrines look so amazing. Then, you find out how many centuries
some have stood. These structures made of huge cuts of timber have survived
battles and fires, unlike those lost to history.

## Black Roofs

The signature black roofs of Japanese shrines comes from the control of
copper oxidation, firing clay with specific coatings or staining woods with
certain oils to reach the dark coloring.

## Colorful Temple

In Kyoto there's a small temple, Yasaka Koshin-do, famous for the
brightly-colored cloth balls hanging in strands. People write their wishes
on the balls and tie them to the temple. If the person gives up one worldly
desire, the gods will grant the wish up to a similar level.
