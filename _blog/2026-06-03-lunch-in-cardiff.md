A short drive west from the Cotswolds put us in [Cardiff][cdf], the capital of
Wales. We came for lunch and a walk through the city's downtown. My wife did
not want to take the long drive to
[Llanfairpwllgwyngyllgogerychwyrndrobwllllantysiliogogogoch][ll], the town
with 58 characters in its name, the longest in Wales and in all of the
United Kingdom.

**Language**

![][l]

Not sure if a recent thing done to keep a language alive or something done
for a long time, but I enjoyed seeing [Welsh][w] alongside English just about
everywhere. I asked around and discovered that about one-quarter of the
population remain conversational in Welsh.

**Smokey Would Approve**

![][t]

Many businesses had water bowls and treats for dogs. This one had quite the
treat buffet set out. Yes, Smokey would approve.

**Cardiff Millennium Centre**

![][m]

In English and Welsh, the face of the building reads, "In these stones
horizons sing." Or, "Creu gwir fel gwydr o ffwrnais awen." The letters are
actually windows, so interior lighting shines outward at night.

**Colored Houses**

![][h]

I noticed that in many areas houses have distinct colors for one another.
