**Bellevue Palace**

Bellevue Palace, located in Berlin's Tiergarten district, has been the
official residence of the president of Germany since 1994.

**St George And The Dragon**

Installed in the 1850s, this bronze sculpture depicts Saint George slaying
a dragon.

![St George][sg]

**Victory Column**

Victoria, the Roman goddess of victory stands twenty-seven feet above the top
of Berlin's Victory Column. She has the nickname *Goldelse*, or
*Golden Lizzy*, a name from a famous German novel. The monument appears in
the Mario Kart Tour and Mario Kart 8 Deluxe video games.

![Victoria][v]
