# Trabant

East German manufacturer VEB Sachsenring produced four models of the *Trabi*
from 1957 until 1991. These front-wheel drive cars contained a two-stroke
engine and featured detachable plastic body panels on a steel unibody frame.
Coined "a spark plug with a roof", more than three million ended up built
and have quite a following of collectors.

Even with so many manufactured, the demand to production ratio topped 40:1
and people routinely waited ten years for a car after paying for one. With
a 0-to-60 timing north of 20 seconds, these did not have much get-up-and-go.
They also topped out in the low 60s mph.

# How About The Tunes?

To quote Mike Damone from [Fast Times At Ridgemont High][ftarh]:

> How about the tunes? The Dream Police da-da-da-da-da-da-da-da."

Seriously though, driving around Berlin in a "different" kind of car, you
should listen to music in an unconventional way. Philips produced the
Auto Mignon between 1958 and 1969, for people to enjoy listening to records
while driving. I spotted one in a Berlin car museum.
