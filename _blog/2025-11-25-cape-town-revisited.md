Anjali and I visited Cape Town back in January 2010. We explored the area
again in late November 2025. Our neighbors joined us for this leg of the
trip, then the four of us met the rest of the folks in Johannesburg, to
start a safari in Zimbabwe, Zambia and Botswana.

## Long Beach (Kommetjie)

We checked into [The Last Word](https://thelastword.co.za/hotel/long-beach),
a cute place to stay in Kommetjie, on the Western Cape. Views open to Long
Beach, a dog-friendly stetch of white sand bordering crystal-blue water.

![Long Beach][longbeach]

## Penguins

A quick drive to Boulder's Beach and we found penguins. My wife had some
trouble getting a good photo, due to the wind.

![Windy day][penguin]

Also, not sure how much Linux runs _under_ the car, but I'm sure a bunch of
the computers _inside_ the car remain penguin-powered.

![Check Under The Car][penguinundercar]

## Table Mountain

Not a fan of the gondola, I remained at the base of Table Mountain while
others ventured to the top. I saw it back in 2010, no reason to head back
up. The best part about staying at the base: beer. A couple of guys seated
next to me asked me if I had ever tried Corona. And, they wanted a photo
taken with me.

![Corona With Friends][beerfriends]

## Cape Point

We drove down to Cape Point. Funny, signs posted all over the place claim
this area as the southernmost point of Africa, but [Cape
Agulhas][capeagulhas] truly has Cape Point beat by more than thirty miles.
Still, the point has nice walking trails and two lighthouses to visit.

![Cape Point][capepoint]
