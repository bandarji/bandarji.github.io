Not too far from the island's airport, you'll find the village of Antimachia.
Set on a plateau, the town offers views unlike any other point on [Kos][kos].

![][castle]

Ruined mostly by a 1926 earthquake, [Antimachia Castle][ac] marks the spot
where knights successfully fought off Ottoman soldiers intent on conquering
the island in 1457. Walking around the place, you get a sense of the grandeur.

Restored, the [Windmill of Antimachia][woa] shows what a traditional 19th
century windmill looked like. This one makes flour used in pastries sold in
their souvenir shop.

![][pea]

Not far from Antimachia, check out [Plaka Forest][pf]. After exploring the
castle ruins, this shaded area provided some reprieve from the heat. The
wooded area has dozens of free-roaming peacocks. We did not spot the all-white
peacock, but we did see photographic evidence offered up by another visitor.
People come here to hike and bike, and to feed the peacocks. One thing about
this area: no services. Bring water!

![][zia]

While in the mountains, we stopped in [Zia][zv], a charming village on the
slopes of Mount Dikeos. The picturesque town has loads of souvenir shops
and eateries. It's most known for the best sunset views from the island, but
we visited at the wrong time of day to view the sun fall into the Aegean Sea.
