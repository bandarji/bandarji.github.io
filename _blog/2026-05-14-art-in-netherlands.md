I rode a bicycle around Amsterdam and we traveled out to a park. In both
cases, I took a few pictures of artwork.

## Wall Street, Part Deux

![][a]

This bull that looks much like the one in New York City. Ah, this sits
in Amsterdam's financial district.

## Dancers Mural

![][b]

Buildings can look cool. Buildings with art on their sides just about always
look cool.

## Against The Tide

![][c]

This memorial sculpture memorializes the murdered Dutch journalist, Peter de
Vries. The Morco mafia, a combination of Dutch and Moroccan criminal families
killed the journalist for coverage that led to arrests within their
organization. I have no idea what the writings state, but the artist calls
the piece "Tegen Alle Stromen In", translated as "Against the Tide".

## Babel

![][d]

The University of Amsterdam officially teaches in twenty-four languages. The
library's exterior quotes the 1934 poem *Awater*, stating "Read it, it doesn't
say what it says."
