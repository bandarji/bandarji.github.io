# Chateau Yaldara

Anjali and I visited [Chateau Yaldara][wwwchateauyaldara], in the
Barossa Valley, just outside Adelaide. They have some lovely fortified
and late harvest wines, in addition to shiraz.

# Grounds Tour

We explored the winery on a self-guided tour.

## Extra For Shipping

Not sure how much this would cost in shipping and handling. Also, our
neighbors would likely throw a fit if they saw this in our yard.

![Big Bottle][bigbottle]

## Cellar

These bottles simply rest atop each other. Yes, they have wine in
