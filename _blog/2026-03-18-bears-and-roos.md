# Bears and Roos

## Koalas

Koalas are not bears. You know why? Because, they are "unkoalafied". I'll see
myself out now.

Koalas do not move much because the eucalyptus they ingest offers very few
calories and takes a lot of energy to digest. In fact, they average twenty
hours of sleep per day.

## Kangaroos

Koalas and kangaroos are symbols of Australia. Kangaroos appear on the
Australian coat of arms and some of the currency. Australian companies
feature kangaroos in commercials, and some even in their logos, such as
[Qantas][qantas].

In the movie [Arrival][arrival], Amy Adam's character relays a myth as truth
to prove a point regarding the communication misunderstandings. In
the common story, then-lieutenant [James Cook][cook] points to kangaroos and
the locals say "kangaroo". He takes it to mean that's what they call the
animals, only to find out much later that "kangaroo" means "I do not
understand."

Fun fact: The collective noun for kangaroos is a mob, a court or a troupe.
