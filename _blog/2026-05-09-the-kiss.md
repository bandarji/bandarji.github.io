# History

Based on a photograph, artist [Dmitri Vrubel][dv] painted the iconic mural
*My God, Help Me To Survive This Deadly Love*, depicting an embrace between
The Soviet and East German leaders, Leonid Brezhnev and Erich Honecker. This
embrace, the [socialist fraternal kiss][sfk], shocked and amused western
journalists then-unfamiliar with the tradition.

# Make Everything Great Again

Street art with the caption *make everything great again* appeared on a BBQ
restaurant in Lithuania, in 2016. The restaurant closed in 2019 and the mural
painted over.
