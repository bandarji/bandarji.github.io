# Cherry Blossoms

We chose this time of year to increase our odds of seeing the beautiful
cherry blossoms. As it turned out, so did just about everyone else. In the
future, I think we shall visit Japan at a different time of year, then go to
Seattle or Washington DC to see the flowers. Do not get me wrong, they ended
up worth the trip, but things got a bit crowded it one cannot predict when
exactly the trees will produce the wonderful colors.
