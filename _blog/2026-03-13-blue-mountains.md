# Katoomba

Katoomba derives from an Aboriginal term for "shiny falling water", and
the town gets this name from its waterfall in the Jamison Valley.

## Hotel Room

Our hotel room at Echoes ended up a fantastic spot. A short walk from
[The Three Sisters][wwwthreesisters], a rock formation with three spires
reaching toward the sky. Legend has it that three sisters fell in love with
three brothers from a neighboring tribe. With that marriage forbidden, the
brothers came to abduct the sisters. Their father turned them to stone, in
order to protect them. He died fighting the brothers. No one could undo the
spell, so the sisters remain rocks atop the mountain cliff.

![Three Sisters][sisters]

in Hindi.

![Hindi Sign][exit]

## Convicts

Near Echo Point, sculptures of convicts building the roadways while
Aboriginal Australians look on. Prisoners would exchange life sentences
elsewhere for twenty-to-thirty years of hard labor in Australia.

![Convicts][convicts]

## Scenic Railway

A tourist attraction in Katoomba named [Scenic World][sw] claims to have the
world's steepest passenger railway. Unfortunately, it does not qualify as a
funicular because it lacks a counterbalancing carriage and uses a winch
system. That stated, the steepest section of track has an incline of 52
degrees! The train carried miners, but converted to a tourist attraction
after the coal mine closure in 1945.

![Train][train]

Scenic World encourages visitors to write messages on chalkboards they
place near the passenger queues. I spotted writings in Hindi and found a note
from a visitor from Texas.

![Hindi][hindi]

![Texas][texas]

## Aussie Punk Music

In town, I found a flyer for a screening of [Harder and Harder][hah], the
sequel to **"The Most Australian Band Ever!"**. Both films focus on the
Sydney punk band, The Hard-Ons. I cannot believe I was so close from a timing
perspective, but could not attend the screening.

![Screeing Flyer][flyer]

Fun fact: Sydney punk band, [Frenzal Rhomb's][frenzalrhomb] album **Hi-Vis
High Tea** has a track named "Ray Ahn Is My Spirit Animal", referring to
Hard-Ons bassist, Raymond Ahn.

On a drive, I spotted this flyer from the Rollins Band 1990 tour of
Australia and New Zealand. Rollins Band formed around Henry Rollins, after
his departure from the infamous Los Angeles punk band, Black Flag.

![Rollins][rollins]
