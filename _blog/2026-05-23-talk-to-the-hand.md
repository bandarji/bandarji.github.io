While I do not know the artist, this large wooden hand somehow represents
philosophies by Ludwig Wittgenstein, an Austrian who lived in Skjolden to get
away from people. He studied mathematics under Bertrand Russell, while working
on his PhD at Cambridge. He died in 1951, at the age of 62, in Cambridge.

Wittgenstein's logic work had a profound effect on computer science. His
writings on logic influenced hardware and software design, and his later
theories on language and rules helped shape machine learning and natural
language processing.
