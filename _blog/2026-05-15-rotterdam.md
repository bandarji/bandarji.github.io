## Cruise Starts

We joined two other couples on a cruise from Dover to Norway, then back again.
On the way from England to Norway, we spent a few hours in Rotterdam,
Netherlands.

It surprised me to learn that Rotterdam means just what it reads. The city's
history started in 1270, when people constructed a dam on the Rotte river.
Well, it took until 1340 before the government recognized the area as a city.
Rotterdam has Europe's largest port, as well.

We did not have a bunch of time to explore, so we hopped on a local trolley
for a loop around town. I captured a few photos.

# Rotterdam

## Kubuswoningen, or Cube Houses

![][b]

Though the walls and windows tilt, the floors remain level. These houses
originated from a concept to build space onto roofs, to more densely populate
urban areas without a need to raze properties in order to construct
apartments. Each contains a little over 1000 square feet (two bed, one bath),
however one-fifth of the space remains fairly unusable due to the tilted
walls.

### Oil Drums

![][e]

Standing more than 25 feet, these fake oil drums form a humanlike figure. The
artist named it *Cascade*, meant to "evoke themes of resource exhaustion,
economic crisis and the fragility of modern consumer culture."

### On The Naughty List

![][f]

The artist intended for Santa to hold up a Christmas tree. Unfortunately,
more than one person has noticed it looks more like something else, having
the artpiece commonly called *Buttplug Gnome*.
