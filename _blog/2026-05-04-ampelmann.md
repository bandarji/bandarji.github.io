The Ampelmännchen, or Ampelmann, a beloved symbol from East Germany, survived
the end of the Iron Curtain. After the Berlin Wall fell, the "little traffic
light man" gained popularity throughout Berlin.

To make traffic light operations apparent to those unable to differentiate
colors, a psychologist created the Ampelman in 1961. Evidently, this
condition affects roughly ten percent of the population. Who knew?

By adding shapes to the lights, anyone who saw the light would understand its
intention. The green light had a cutout of a walking Ampelmann, the red a man
with his arms outstretched, legs together.
