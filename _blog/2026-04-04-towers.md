# Towers

## Japan Radio Tower

Inspired by the Eiffel Tower and painted orange for safety regulations, the
Japan Radio Tower, better known as Tokyo Tower climbs more than 1,000 feet
into the air. It was the tallest in Japan until the completion of Tokyo
Skytree.
