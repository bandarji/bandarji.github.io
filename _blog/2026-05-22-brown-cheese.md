Norway's *brunost*, or *brown cheese*, tastes great!

Made by boiling down whey until the milk sugars caramelize, it sits
somewhere between cheese and fudge. Firm enough to slice, the cheese melts in
your mouth.

On a slice of bread, or melting over waffles, it tastes sweet and a
little nutty. Not quite dessert, but something like the dairy aisle added a
candy.

If you ever get a chance, by all means try this.
