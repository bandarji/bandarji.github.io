![Relaxation][phat]

Ah, just what the doctor ordered...

Here, I relax on the balcony of our Cabo hotel room. A view of the waves, a
gentle breeze and plenty of sunshine. I moved my hat down to cover my feet in
an attempt to keep them from burning.

![Lizard][pliz]

Anjali made a friend at the pool. Well, sort of. This lizard came to see what
we had ordered for lunch and that sent Anjali running for the hills.

Funny enough, I only captured a handful of photos over the week. Maybe the
universe told me to chill and enjoy my time in Mexico. ¡Supongo que eso podría
ser cierto!
