# Carlsbad

Roughly thirty miles north of San Diego sits Carlsbad, known for beaches and
golf. Some refer to Carlsbad as *Titanium Valley*, because of its many golf
courses and home to the Callaway, Cobra, TaylorMade and Titleist companies.

For other fun, the city has ties to skateboarding, home to one of the first
skateparks, built in 1976. After its closing, [Del Mar Skate Ranch][dmsr],
made famous by frequent visitors Tony Hawk, Christian Hosoi, Rodney Mullen and
many others skating innovators. Carlsbad now has several maintained
skateparks.

Legoland California opened in 1999, the first Lego-themed park outside of
Europe. The park remains Carlsbad's second largest employer. Carlsbad also
stands as the corporate headquarters location for a number of well-known
companies, including Jenny Craig, Rockstar Games, Upper Deck, Jazzercise and
Hot Dog on a Stick.

# MarBrisa

We stayed at the Hilton Grand Vacations property called [MarBrisa][mb]. If you
want a great place to stay near San Diego, check this place out. While we did
take advantage of the offerings of the resort, including multiple pools and
its bar and grill, it met our access and entertainment needs. Close to the
highway, we could get in and out easily. The couple that traveled with us have
family in the area, and one nephew stopped by for drinks and snacks one
evening.

# Honolulu Clipper

The aviation decor in the Carlsbad [Ruby's Diner][rd] includes a model of the
Honolulu Clipper. Pan American Airways built the flying boat prototype for
airmail service, eventually adding passengers in the late 1930s. It joined
the US Navy during WWII, ending its service during Operation Magic Carpet.
Forced to land 650 miles short of Oahu after engine failure, the Clipper ended
up intentionally sunk by gunfire after a successful passenger rescue
operation.

![Honolulu Clipper][hc]

# Out of Gas (Sort Of)

On the way home from Carlsbad to Reno, we ran into a snag. Google Maps points
to an EV charging station, however the station sits inside the
[Honda Proving Center's][hp] campus, not open to the public. Unable to make it
to the next station, in Mojave, we ended up on the side of the highway. Had to
get the Rivian towed into town. The dashboard informed me that we had two
miles of charge remaining when I plugged it in. Fun times!
