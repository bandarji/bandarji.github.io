# Ísafjörður

Iceland stop number two brought us to Ísafjörður. We probably should have
joined an excursion, as walking the town did not provide much of an
experience.

![Beauty][i2]

Views on the way in and out of the port were spectacular.

![Airport][i3]

The local airstrip was interesting, right on the water. Planes have to
snake between mountains in order to use the airport.

![Kids Swimming][i4]

I have no idea what the water temperature was, but these kids enjoyed jumping
in.

![Old House][i5]

A yard full of boats: decoration or junk?

![Tunnel Faces][i6]

This walkway tunnel had some cool art painted on the walls.

# Back on the Ship

![Vista][i7]

Halfway through the cuise, I *finally* took a pic of the cruise ship.

![Indian Meal][i8]

One of the couples we met on the cruise actually lives on a cruise ship. They
know the ins and outs of cruise life. So, they arranged for a custom Indian
meal. People seated around us, smelling the delicious food, wanted to know
how they could do similar.
