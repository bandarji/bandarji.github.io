# Greek Island of Kos

![][banner]

We joined family from London and Delhi on the Greek island of [Kos][kos].
Just under 40,000 people live on Greece's third largest island. We all
stayed at [Mitsis Blue Domes][mbd] resort.

## Beaches And Shorelines

![][paradise]

Driving around the island, we stopped at [Paradise Beach][ipb1], a beach with
snack huts, cocktail bars, clean sand and crystal-blue water. An interesting
feature of one part of the beach: bubbles. Volcanic activity causes gas to
seep through the ocean floor, visible as tiny bubbles in the shallows.

For some reason, we only captured a selfie at this [Kefalos Bay][wkb] stop.

![][w1]

The blue water bordered by even bluer water looked so inviting!

![][w2]

Much of Kos' coastline has high rocky "hills" that fall into the ocean rather
than gentle grades into the water. The beaches do offer long stretches of
flat sand, but we only found a dozen or so around the entire island.

![][bp]

The small villages on and near the beaches offer great spots for quick,
delicious seafood. Seems like every place has a decommissioned boat out front.

## Protected Waters

![][w3]

On the eastern side of the island, we came across this "beach", with its
manmade jetty to ensure calm waters. You can see the row of beach umbrellas on
the left side of the photo, for scale.
