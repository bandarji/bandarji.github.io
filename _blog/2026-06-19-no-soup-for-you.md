![Clan Campbell][i2]

I hear that I'm a descendant of Lord and Lady Campbell. Not an ancestry buff,
I just believe this without tracing any lineage. That stated, the castle cafe
would not give me a free bowl of soup.

![Inveraray Castle][i1]

The chiefs of Clan Campbell resided at [Inveraray Castle][ic]. We visited. The
drive there from Glasgow was quite beautiful, though the rains arrived during
the short trip.

![David Stirling Statue][i4]

We spotted this statue on the side of the "highway". Really, more like just
off from a two-lane-ish roadway. The statue is of [David Stirling][ds],
who founded the [Special Air Service][sas].

![Crocs][i3]

I mentioned the rain. That rain kept us from visiting Stirling Castle and a
couple of other places. So, we had lunch at [River House][rhr]. Approaching
the entrance, we saw this warning, to look out for crocodiles. Oh, there they
are!
