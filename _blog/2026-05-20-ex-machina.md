Alex Garland's 2014 science fiction film, *Ex Machina*, follows a programmer
invited to the home of the company CEO to administer a turing test to a
female android. The CEO's home, the Juvet Landscape Hotel in real life, rests
in the hills only a few miles from where we stopped for coffee.

![][b]

According to sixteenth century myth, a man named Gudbrand jumped this gorge to
escape a mob set to kill him for marrying a local lady. He leaped and lived
his remaining days safe above the valley, because no one dared to jump after
him. That included his bride.

This location now has a lovely place for a bite and a drink, called Gudbrandsjuvet cafe (right side of photo).

![][a]

The bridge at the top of the photo permits people to walk over this gorge.
