# Tomfoolery

Driving in the Barossa Valley, we happened upon [Tomfoolery
Wines][tomfoolery], in the town of Light Pass. Not only do they have
great-tasting wine, but they have some of the coolest label artwork.
Pen-drawn pistols and pirates to women and wineglasses hang on the
walls of their tasting room. So serious, they even named their cellar
club "Fools and Friends".
