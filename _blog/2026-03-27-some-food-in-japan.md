# Eating And Drinking

## NOPE

Tired of hearing claims that highly-sweetened and caffinated drinks are
unhealthy, Suntory released [Guilty Carbonated NOPE][gcnope]. According to
the label, The flavor comes from a blend of "99 Enchanting Fruits & Spices".
You get 600ml instead of the normally-available 500ml bottling. No one can
agree on what it tastes like, with a combination of sweet, sour, bitter,
salty and umami notes.

![NOPE Soda][nope]

**Nene Chicken**

This place was pretty amazing. You want something spicy? [Nene Chicken][nc]
(Korean: 네네치킨) has what you want. Look at the red pepper coating this crispy
chicken!

## Sakura Festival

We happened upon the [Sakura Festival][hanami], which celebrates the cherry
blossom blooming. In Ueno, vendors set up booths and people jump from spot to
spot, sampling food and drink.

**Mexican Food**

Carnitas tacos have made it to Japan! They also had a sign that read, "We
have tequila," so you know it's legit.
