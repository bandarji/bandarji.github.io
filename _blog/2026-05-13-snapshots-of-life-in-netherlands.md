# Life In Netherlands

Obviously, I only got a glimpse at life in Netherlands, but I took a few pics
to show what I came to understand. I would very much like to revisit the
small towns.

## Zwolle

We stayed near the small town of [Zwolle][zwww], Netherlands, an hour by car
or train from [Amsterdam][awww].

### Coffee

![][coffee]

First stop: coffee! Not sure where to go, I figured how could you go wrong
with a shop selling Columbian roast that has a Jeep facade?

### People

![][sv]

Zwolle's narrow streets would have a car coming through every so often, but
most often you'd only see bicyclists weaving through pedestrians.

![][ho]

This coffee shop had its seating positioned so that folks aimed their
attention to people walking the street, rather than directly toward each
other. I thought that made for great people watching; truly just hanging out.

## Amsterdam

![][db]

**Bicycles!** Wow, the bicycling in Amsterdam feels like a simulation of a
bee roaming around a hive. I actually rented a bike to explore the city a bit.
And no, I did not kill that bicycle.

### Leaning Buildings

![][cb]

Many of Amsterdam's buildings lean toward the streets or canals. A beam at
the top of the building usually has a hook, so goods would get hoisted up
through windows instead of going up stairs. The lean would help prevent goods
from smashing windows as some swing would occur no matter how much care went
into hoisting.

An interesting note about Amsterdam buildings: the width of the face of a
property mattered for taxation purposes. So, many buildings would remain
narrow. A wealthy homeowner or pretentious businessman would limit the
number of floors and build wide, to show off their status.

### Red Light District

Areas of cities designated or otherwise well known for a concentration of
sex workers, sex shops and strip clubs go by the term **red list districts**.
The source of the term remains up in the air, with several competing stories
regarding the origin, but the first known written use comes from Sandusky,
Ohio.

In Amsterdam, the De Wallen area of the city houses the largest red light
district. There, sex workers display themselves from viewing boxes lit in
red, to passers by on the street.

![][cs]

The area does not permit photography, with monitoring cameras and posted
signs showing hefty fines for violations. Surrounding the sex workers' places
of business, many shops and museums exist, like this condom shop.

### Park Anywhere

![][sc]

An example of the best of intentions that ended up a horrible idea: these
tiny cars. To provide handicapped persons with convenient mobility around the
city, the government subsidized purchases of these small conveyances. People
could drive them in the bicycle lanes and park them anywhere. Unfortunately,
able people started paying money to doctors in order to claim disabilities so
they could travel around the city without the inconveniences of biking in the
weather or paying for parking. Now, these tiny cars cost a small fortune and
the government estimates that less than 50% of the owners actually need them.

### The Canals

![][fm]

Everything in Amsterdam revolves around the canal waterways. Many businesses
rest of barges rather than off the streets. Above, a chain of barges serve
as a flower market.

![][dp]

Do not add your own fluids to the canals. Doing so comes with a sizable fine.
Every so many blocks, a public urinal exists, for relief. That stated, I do
not know if the city accommodated women by adding public toilets.
