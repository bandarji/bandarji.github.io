![Puffins][i1]

The boat ride out to the puffins: nice. The boat ride back: not so nice. It
rained cats, dogs and puffins!

![Harbor][i2]

After the rain left me wet and cold, it felt great to come back into the
harbor.

![Coast Guard][i3]

The Icelandic Coast Guard has only four active ships. Here's one, but I did
not get the name of the vessel in the photo. Based on the shape, I believe
this is the Þór (Thor). Appropriate, because we visited Reykjavik on a
Thursday.

![Þúfa art installation][i4]

Icelandic artist [Ólöf Nordal][wwwon] created a spot of serenity and
meditation in the Reykjavik harbor. Þúfa is a manmade grassy mound with a
fish-drying shed on top. People walk up using the steps to enjoy the view.

We skipped visiting, due to th rain.

![Crabby Car Lot][i5]

I liked the crab painted on the lot, at the harbor.

![Le Kock][i6]

To warm ourselves, we visited [Le Kock][wwwlk] for coffee and a pastry. They
had some cool artwork.

![Island][i7]

Afterward, we went back to the ship. Once aboard, I did not feel like getting
back into town. Not even a record store visit would have coaxed me back
ashore.
