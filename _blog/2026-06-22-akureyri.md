# Back in Iceland

![View From The Hilltop][i1]

Back in 2018, we and another couple rented a car and drove the island. On this
cruise, we pulled into three ports. First up, Akureyri.

![Heart Signal][i2]

This town's traffic signals have heart patterns for the red lights. How cute.

![Garden][i3]

We walked and walked and walked to the [Lystigarðinum][lg]. Gonna say, not
worth it. It was green. It was expansive. It was far from the port.

![Face Mural][i4]

Close to port, a cluster of shops and eateries exists. This mural caught my
eye.

![McBabyCorpse][i5]

I should have purchased *My Pussy Is Hungry*, a book of funny comic strips
that I spotted at a book store. Why? Well, it had some funny entries. More
importantly, it goes for $350 [on Amazon][oa]!

# Back Onboard

![Canopies][i6]

On Day 9 of the 22-day cruise, we finally took advantage of having a butler.
We requested drinks and snacks. Why did we wait so long?

![Cooks][i7]

The kitchen prepared Indian food this night. The cooks had a good time
laughing at my horrible Hindi.
