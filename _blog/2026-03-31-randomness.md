Here's a few pictures from Japan that I could not find a good category for.

**Security Robot**

Not sure what this security robot can actually do in any security situation,
but it's doing its best to look tough.

![Security Robot][secbot]

**Tattoos**

Because of the use of tattoos to identify Japanese mafia members, some places
do not allow people into their establishments with tattoos showing.

![Tattoos][tattoos]

**Photoshoot**

Not sure what this photoshoot was for, but it was something professional.

![Photoshoot][photoshoot]

**Dogs And Cats Living Together**

Since many people cannot keep a pet where they live, some cafes and other
businesses have dogs and cats inside. People can freely pet the animals while
working on their laptops, sipping tea or reading books.

![Dogs][nodog]
