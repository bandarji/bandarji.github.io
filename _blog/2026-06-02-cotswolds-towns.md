**Bourton House**

![][c02]

More than 300,000 people visit [Bourton-on-the-Water][botw] every year. The
small town of 3,500 residents maintain a certain look and feel of natural
beauty and old-world charm. Here, a typical home sits beside the village's
river.

**Welcome To Venice**

![][c03]

Low, arched stone bridges cross the river at various points in the village,
the oldest has a construction date of 1654. People have nicknamed the town the
"Venice of the Cotswolds".

**Shelley's Walk**

![][c08]

The churchyard beside the [St Lawrence Church][slc] resulted in the poem
*A Summer Evening Churchyard, Lechlade*. Percy and Mary Shelley visited
Lechlade in the summer of 1815 and a sunset viewing inspired Percy to pen the
famous work. The footpath into the cemetary behind the church bears the name
*Shelley's Walk*.
