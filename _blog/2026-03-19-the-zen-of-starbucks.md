# The Zen Of Starbucks

## Zen

The word Zen comes from the Japanese pronunciation of the Chinese word 禪,
which comes from the Sanskrit word ध्यान. These translate as "meditative
state" or "concentration", from the ultimate knowlege of truth discovered by
Buddha. This truth remains so profound that words cannot express its meaning.
Instead, understanding truth comes through meditation under the guidance of a
Zen master. Once understood, this truth frees the mind from ignorance and
suffering.

Roughly, if not crudely, Zen gets used worldwide to represent anything calm
or peaceful. In this post, I shall use Zen as the Japanese presented it to
me. They described a Kyoto Starbucks as the most Zen Starbucks in the world.
