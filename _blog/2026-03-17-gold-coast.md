# Gold Coast

Less than one hour's drive from Brisbane, Gold Coast has nice beaches and
water activities for Aussie and foreign visitors. We rented a condo on the
"Glitter Strip" for a few nights. A neighbor of ours in Hawaii has a
daughter who lives here, so it was great to meet up with her and her husband,
having met them in Hawaii before.

Here's the view from our balcony.

![Gold Coast][gc]

We went shopping. I was fascinated by the golf carts shuttling folks around.

![Shopping Mall][mall]

The coffee shop downstairs from our condo had a few records to spin.

![Music][music]

One funny thing: When we went to supper one night, I ordered carbonara. They
brought me a gigantic spoon.

![Spoon][spoon]

Speaking of supper, we ate a place down the road, in Broadbeach. After we
finished our meal, the waiter gave us a small container with fish food, to
throw out into the harbor.

![Fish Food][fish]
