# Just For Fun

## Refill Your Body

In 1965, Florida Gators football coach Ray Graves requested a drink to
replenish fluids lost by players from sweating. A team of University of
Florida scientists came up with what we now call Gatorade. The drink
basically made sweat taste good enough to drink.

In Japan, they don't sugar coat it. Here's "sweat".

![Sweat][sweat]

## Don Quijote

One place you might want to pop into while in Japan: Don Quijote. The
discount store has everything from groceries to clothes to electronics to
souvenirs. With hundreds of locations worldwide and nearly 200 in Japan, this
store has a massive following.

![Don Quijote][signdonq]

AKA Donki.

![Donki][signdonki]
