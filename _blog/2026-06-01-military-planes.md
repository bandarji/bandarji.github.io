![][u2a]

[RAF Fairford][raf] sits not far from the Cotswolds. We watched jets flying
in and out of the base. With the [2026 Iran War][iw] going, increased activity
had photographers surrounding the base's perimeter, capturing photos of
aircraft.

In addition to photographers, some protestors sat outside the base's fencing,
upset with the UK Prime Minister's permission granted to the US military to
use the base for war-time operations. Local residents have also complained
about disruptions. Heavy bombers, like the [B-52 Stratofortress][b52] and
[B-1 Lancer][b1l], have set off car alarms and shook houses.

![][u2b]

Here, a [Lockheed U-2][lu2] reconnaissance aircraft comes in for a landing.
The "Dragon Lady" climbs to a high altitude, above 70,000 feet, to spy on
ground activity.
