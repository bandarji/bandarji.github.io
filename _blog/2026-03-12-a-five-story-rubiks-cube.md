# The d'Arenberg Cube

A short drive from Adelaide, Australia, in the McLaren Vale wine region, sits
[The d'Arenberg Cube][dacube]. Inspired by [Rubik's Cube][rcube] and the
puzzling art of winemaking, Chester Osborn (fourth generation owner),
designed the building in 2003. Construction of the five-story building
wrapped in 2017, opening to the public late that year.

We visited early, before they open, to tour the building and its 25 Salvador
Dali sculptures. After that, we sampled wines. I recommend following our lead
because it made for a great, fun day.
