Not much to see here, and perhaps that was the point. Leirvik is just a small
town in Norway. It's real life outside the majestic fjords and impressive
mountain ranges.

We visited a cooking supplies store and Anjali purchased some items for our
dining room. We also tried a waffle with brown cheese.
