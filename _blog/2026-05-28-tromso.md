![][a]

In Tromsø, I had a specific place to visit: Hard Rock Cafe. After purchasing a
guitar-shaped magnet from the northernmost Rock Shop, I could relax and
explore the city.

On the way, I found the Blå Rock Cafe: not the right place and they did not
sell magnets.

![][b]

[Polaria][pwww], the most northerly aquarium on the planet, resides in
Tromsø. The building represents ice floes pressed against land.

![][c]

We also visited the Troll Museum, a worthwhile stop if you find yourself in
Tromsø.
