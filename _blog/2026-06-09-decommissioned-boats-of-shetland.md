[Shetland][she], the Scottish archipelago that marks the northernmost region
of the United Kingdom, gets its name from the Norse word *hjalt*, meaning
"hilt". With a population under 25,000 people and with a single burgh, 
[Lerwick][lw], the islands only have three settlements with a population
over 500.

Quite the dog and pony show, Shetland has its own breeds of, well, dogs and
ponies. Due to the remote nature of the island chain, animals distinct to
Shetland include the [Shetland pony][pony], [Shetland sheepdog][dog],
[Shetland cow][cow], [Shetland pig][pig], [Shetland goose][goose],
[Shetland duck][duck] and [Shetland sheep][sheep].

For this blog entry, though, I want to focus on another peculiar beast, the
decommissioned boat. Shetland residents have come up with creative ways to
use boats no longer seaworthy.
