![Natural Deep Harbor][p1]

Falmouth, named so because it is the mouth of the River Fal, sits in Cornwall,
England. Its natural harbor remains one of the world's deepest, perfect for
sea trade and tourism. Interesting note: Falmouth, Jamaica, was named after
the Cornwall town because the Jamaican governor responsible for its
establishment was born there.

Just outside the port, one shop-lined street sells just about anything and
everything a tourist would want to purchase. That is, if they want the same
souvenirs as most tourists. That stated, I did pop into some interesting
shops and spotted a few interesting things.

**Shark Mural**

![Shark Mural][p5]

Artist [Marc Craig][wwwmc] created [The Ark of Extinction][wwwtaoe] project
to raise awareness for endangered animals. His basking shark mural in
Falmouth works as intended. I mean, it got me to look up information about
the vulnerable shark population. Several others stopped to consider the
mural and I noticed a number of them opening their phones to look up more
information.

Check out this [video][wwwbsa] showing how the artist creates fine-detail
doodles of animals.

**Johnny Guitar**

![Movie Poster][p2]

Joan Crawford starred in the 1954 western, [Johnny Guitar][wwwjg]. I have not
watched the movie, but guitarist [John 5][wwwj5] sampled audio from the film
for his song ["Welcome To Violence"][wwwwtv].

**Pie Hard**

![Pie Hard][p4]

Apparently, *Pie Hard* is not a Christmas festival. It's **the** Christmas
festival! Visit Falmouth in December for pie, beer and Christmas.
Yippe-pie-yay!

**The Art Remains Outside The Gallery**

![View][p6]

Since 1989, [Beside The Wave Gallery][wwwbtwg] has sold contemporary art. The
four ladies who operate the gallery picked a perfect spot. While the art
caught my attention, the view out the gallery's back window kept it.

Take a peek at [their Instagram profile][wwwbtwgi] for pics of their art and
events.
