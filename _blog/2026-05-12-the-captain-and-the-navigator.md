# Giethoorn

A small village with very, very few roads, Giethoorn has 170 bridges for
people to walk and bike over canals. We rented an electric boat on the edge
of town to explore the waterways. Enjoying the afternoon, moving along at
a snail's pace, made it to my top five favorite things about Netherlands.
