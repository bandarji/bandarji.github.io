# Flying Foxes

Founded as a penal settlement in the early 19th century, Port Mac now has
over 50,000 free residents. And, more than 250,000 flying foxes! The black
dots in the sky in the picture below shows bunches of fruit bats flying. At
dusk, they fly from their colonies to feed. These animals can have a
wingspan exceeding three feet!

![Flying Foxes][bats]

## An Old-ish Lighthouse

This lighthouse did not make it onto Australia's top ten oldest. The cute
little lighthouse at Tacking Point comes in at number thirteen.

![Tacking Point Lighthouse][lighthouse]

It does look out on a nice view of the ocean.

![Ocean View From Tacking Point][tackingpointview]

The view from our hotel room wasn't bad, by comparison. The Rydges Hotel
ended up pretty well located for us to enjoy Port Mac's lively downtown.

![Hotel Room View][roomview]

## A Walk By The River

The Hastings River has a paved walkway on the Port Mac side. People have
painted some of the rocks over the years.

People kayaking in the river, with a few painted rocks in the foreground.

![Hastings River Painted Rocks][r1]

*"Maybe you're the lighthouse in someone else's storm."* Sounds like the
type of thing Ted Lasso might say.

![Hastings River Painted Rocks][r2]

An Ironman team painted this one. I like the quote from the movie Young Guns.

![Hastings River Painted Rocks][r3]

It's a green dragon.
