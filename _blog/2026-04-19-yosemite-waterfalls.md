# Yosemite Waterfalls

On the way to [San Diego][sdt], we decided to stop in
[Yosemite National Park][ywww]. The National Park Service website claims that
Yosemite's waterfalls remain its best known feature. They certainly did not
disappoint.

## Bridalveil Fall

The Ahwahneechee called this waterfall a name that translates to *Spirit of
the Puffing Wind*, because the water flow would at times not reach the ground.
A newspaper editor in 1856 suggested the name Bridalveil Fall due to its
appearance which mimicked a lacy veil. These conditions come from strong winds
that can blow on the side of the falling water.

![Bridalveil Fall][wf1]

## Yosemite Falls

Spilling water nearly 2,500 feet, no park waterfall stands taller than
Yosemite Falls. This waterfall ceases it flow in autumn. Some rock climbers
have dared an ascent up the face beneath the falls, a feat considered
extremely dangerous, as the flow can restart unexpectedly.

**Streams**

In the spring, the streams flow quite well. Everywhere you turn, another
stream appears. The background sounds of water trickling and whooshing about
add to the enjoyment of short walks through the wilderness.
