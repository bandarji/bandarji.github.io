# Selling Punk Duplicates

Collecting records for a long time, I have ended up with a few duplicates.
This happens for several reasons. One, a repressing comes along that I buy
for the extras, like a photo booklet, alternate artwork or included single.
Two, I merge a purchased collection with my own. And three, I purchase a box
set to complete an artist collection.

Before I list these on Discogs or eBay, I wanted to let a few Facebook groups
and subreddits get first dibs.
