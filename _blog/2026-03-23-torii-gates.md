# Torii Gates

The Japanese call the iconic red gates at some Shinto shrines *torii*. The
styling comes from the Indian torana (Sanskrit: तोरण). Perhaps the most
famous torii appears as a path leading up Mount Inari to the Fushimi
Inari-taisha shrine, in Kyoto. Approximately 800 torii placed closely
together form a tunnel up to the main Inari shrine, with thousands more
situated around the mountain, between shrines.

Cold and rainy, we did not spend a lot of time walking through the tunnel
formed by the gates. The few pictures snapped under the torii did not turn
out well, either.
